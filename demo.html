What the feature does

Community Bot keeps combined subscriber roles in step with the server's Twitch subscriber and YouTube membership integration roles. The records below show the Tier 1 role, Loot Goblin (Tier 1 Sub); the bot also has Tier 2 and Tier 3 mappings. It adds the combined role when a member has a qualifying integration role and removes it when none are left. Every change carries the audit-log reason Community Bot: combined tier sub role sync.

The full version checks every server member at startup and reacts when a member's roles change, even if that member never uses the bot. Discord's Guild Members intent is being requested only to bring back that server-wide sync. Presence and Message Content are not requested.

Limits of this evidence

  • It proves the role changes happened, not what started each one. A role-change audit record does not say whether the bot was reacting to a member update, running its startup check, or refreshing someone who was using the bot. This report does not claim any single change came from a privileged member event.
  • Server-wide member-event sync is off right now. Discord removed the app's privileged intents. On October 18, 2026 at 11:04 AM PDT the app's privileged-intent flags read 0. After recovery later that morning, the bot now runs in a reduced mode: it can still sync people it sees through voice events, slash commands, and one-at-a-time member lookups, but not the whole server.
  • All 10 records are timestamped before that 11:04 AM PDT check. They show historical role changes, not a live test of the currently disabled server-wide member-event handler.
  • This is a summary of API records, not a screenshot of Discord's Audit Log screen. It is supporting evidence, and Discord may not accept it as the demo it asks for.
